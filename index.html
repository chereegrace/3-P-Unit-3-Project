Brownie batter recipe #1👩🏾‍🍳:

  1. 1/2 cup unsalted butter melted (113g)
  2. 1/3 cup cocoa powder (35g)
  3. 1/2 cup grandulated sugar (100g)
  4. 1/2 cup packed light or dark brown sugar (110g)
  5. 1/2 teaspoon salt
  6. 2 large eggs room temperature
  7. 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  8. 1/2 cup all-purpose flour (6og)
  9. 3/4 cup semisweet chocolate chips (135g)
